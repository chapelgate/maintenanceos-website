FAQs

At Utilita, we believe in smarter fairer energy – here’s what that means for our customers:

  • Our award-winning app helps you take control of your consumption, and save energy with personalised tips.
  • No Standing Charge on our Pay As You Go tariffs – only pay for what you use.
  • Enter exclusive prize draws to win cash, vouchers, tech, tickets and more with Utilita Extra.
  • Get in-person support on the high street in our Energy Hubs.

You can start your switch to Utilita here.

Complete our online form by telling us a little about your home, and we’ll offer you a quote in 30 seconds.

If you want to go ahead with your quote, we’ll take care of the switching process from start to finish. There’s no need to tell your old supplier, we’ll take care of that – but they might ask you for a final meter reading.

Switching energy suppliers usually takes no more than five working days.

If you prefer, you can choose a switch date further in advance.

None of Utilita’s Pay As You Go tariffs include a Standing Charge, which means you’ll only pay for the energy you actually use.

If you’re eligible for the Warm Home Discount (WHD) and Utilita was your energy supplier on 23 August 2026, we’ll be responsible for your WHD payment.

We’ll start making payments from January 2027, and the scheme will end for the year on 31 March 2027.
