More about us

As a leading merchant in plumbing, heating and cooling Wolseley has supported the trade for decades. With unrivalled expert know-how and first-class customer support Wolseley offers great value you can trust.

Always delivering on our promises we help our trade customers get the job done right first time with reliable supply of quality products.

Our brands

Plumb Centre is the UKs largest trade specialist in heating, bathrooms, and plumbing. Knowledgeable service, simple ordering and our stock of industry leading plumbing and heating brands online and in branch supports businesses in keeping projects on track and helping get the job done.

With over 400 local branches nationwide, expert knowledge and stock is always close by.

"Plumb Centre is a trusted supplier of plumbing and heating supplies. We have been working with them for many years and have always been impressed with their quality products and competitive prices." - Plumbing and heating contractor

Pipe Centre is the UK's leading pipe specialist, offering an extensive range of heating, commercial plumbing, and drainage systems from industry-leading brands. Our experts provide exceptional customer service, covering everything from specification and estimation to sales support and site clearance.

With over 40 branches nationwide, you can be sure to find the expert knowledge and products you need to keep your projects on track.

"Pipe Centre is a one-stop shop for all of our pipe and fitting needs. Their staff is always knowledgeable and helpful, and they go the extra mile to make sure we have what we need to get the job done." - Industrial plumbing contractor

Climate Centre is one of the UK's leading specialists in air conditioning, HVAC, and refrigeration, with the largest network of specialist cooling branches across the country. We provide a full range of air conditioning and refrigeration equipment, accessories, and spares from the world's leading brands. Our experts are on-hand to support you with everything you need to get the job done, whether that's product advice or a customer refrigeration build.

Supporting Trades

Wolseley is committed to providing trade customers with the best possible service and support. We understand that trade customers need to be able to rely on their suppliers to provide them with the products and services they need when they need them. That's why Wolseley offers a wide range of benefits and services to make life easier for their trade customers.

Trade account holders at Wolseley enjoy:

  • Personalised trade prices
  • Access to 300K products
  • Click and collect from over 450 branches
  • Free access to PartsArena & our product selector tools
  • Trade Credit account or pay as you go with a Trade Cash account
  • Full online order tracking
  • Create your own job lists

There is also a range of services to take advantage of, including technical support, training courses, installation services and project management support.
