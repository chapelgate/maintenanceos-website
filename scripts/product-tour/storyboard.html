How a call is designed to work

AI collects and classifies. Rules own safety and money.

After early access, the product is designed so AI assists at intake. Authority for safety and money stays with system rules and people.

1 · Intake

Collect and classify

Designed to collect caller details and classify what it hears in UK property-maintenance language — including 999 guidance where relevant. Voice intake is under testing.

2 · Controlled triage

Rules hold the line

Tenant isolation, lifecycle transitions, safety latching, escalation queues, and approvals sit in server rules. Consequential actions are not left to the model.

3 · Human oversight

People keep judgement

Explicit-request human transfer and human clearance keep judgement with your team. No autonomous dispatch, booking, or payment claims.

Status: Designed behaviour — not a live always-on line. The model does not override safety or execute money movement.

Built for UK property maintenance

Operations language — not a generic chatbot.

Multi-tenant properties, residents, contractors, work orders, jobs, and call sessions exist in product source. Capabilities are under testing for release.

Tenant-scoped ops

Properties, residents, contractors, work orders / jobs, and communications stay scoped to the organisation.

UK 999 — never 911

UK terminology and 999 emergency guidance in product source and regressions. Never 911.

Structured intake

The receptionist layer is designed to collect caller details. It must not invent bookings, dispatches, or safety assurances.

Money stays out

No payment, refund, compensation, fee-waiver, or client-money execution authority — for the model or automated movement.

What we will not claim

Not a public always-on live phone.

Pilot-stage. Core controls are real; public always-on phone is not declared ready yet. Further controlled live testing is still required.

  • No fabricated customers, logos, or awards
  • No ISO, SOC 2, or bank-grade claims we have not earned
  • No “handles N concurrent lines” marketing numbers
  • No public live receptionist claim until that gate is cleared
