# Terms and Conditions — G-Cloud Submission

## Basis
Based on G-Cloud 15 Framework Agreement (consolidates Cloud Compute 2 and G-Cloud 14 Lots 1-3 and 4). Framework agreement published by Government Commercial Agency (GCA), successor to Crown Commercial Service (CCS) from 1 April 2026.
Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/news/g-cloud-relaunches-with-biggest-upgrade-in-its-history; https://www.gov.uk/guidance/g-cloud-templates-and-legal-documents (accessed 15 Nov 2026)

## Applicable terms
- G-Cloud 15 Framework Agreement (template from GCA)
- Call-Off Contract per service purchased
- Chapelgate standard SaaS terms (supplementary, not conflicting with Framework)

## Key terms

### Service availability
- Target: 99.5% monthly uptime (excluding planned maintenance)
- Planned maintenance window: Sundays 02:00-06:00 UTC (notified 5 working days prior)
- SLA credits per Call-Off Contract terms

### Data protection
- Data controller: Customer (public sector buyer)
- Data processor: Chapelgate (supplier)
- UK GDPR / DPA 2018 compliant
- Data processed in UK / EEA only
- Sub-processors: none (all services run on Chapelgate infrastructure)
- Data returned/deleted within 30 days of contract end

### Security
- TLS 1.2+ in transit
- Encryption at rest (AES-256)
- Cyber Essentials self-assessment complete (certification fee parked — Andy-only)
- SBOMs provided per product
- Penetration test reports available on request (no live pen test yet — Andy-only parked)

### Intellectual property
- Customer owns their data
- Chapelgate owns platform IP
- Source code not escrowed (not required for G-Cloud)

### Liability
- Liability capped at 12 months' fees per Call-Off Contract (standard G-Cloud)
- No liability for indirect/consequential loss

### Term & termination
- Call-off contracts up to 60 months (G-Cloud 15 max)
- 90 days' notice for non-renewal
- Immediate termination for material breach

### Support
- Standard support: business hours (Mon-Fri 09:00-17:00 UK), email/ticket, 2 business day response
- Enterprise support: extended hours, 4 hour response, dedicated contact

### Changes
- Terms changes require mutual written agreement
- Framework terms prevail over supplementary terms
