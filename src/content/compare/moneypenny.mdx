## Detailed comparison

### Product category
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch UK maintenance operations software with AI-assisted intake. The AI receptionist capability is under testing. Fixed rules and authorised human clearance keep control of safety and money. Not commercially generally available; not a public always-on phone service.
- **Moneypenny**: Founded 2000. "World's leading outsourced communications partner". 1,000+ US and UK team members. 10,000s of businesses. 40,000+ calls handled daily (source: moneypenny.com homepage JSON-LD, accessed 2026-10-19). Offers both human phone answering and AI Receptionist.

### AI capability
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: AI extracts structured repair facts from natural language (website scanner + voice). Safety latches hold dangerous/high-value/high-risk items for authorised human decision. Voice gate: low-confidence fields reported as "Unknown — not found".
- **Moneypenny AI Receptionist**: "Ultra realistic with the option to escalate to real people built in. Emotionally intelligent with smooth human-like flow. Industry-leading response speed. Follows pre-agreed call flows to complete tasks" (source: moneypenny.com/us/ai-receptionist, accessed 2026-10-19). Also offers "MessageMaker" — AI that "auto-drafts full and accurate summaries ready to be shared instantly" (source: moneypenny.com homepage). Hybrid model: "Real people and AI deliver seamless experiences for your business 24/7".

### Safety architecture
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Explicit safety latches (dangerous work, high value, gas/electrical, safeguarding). Human clearance required. Audit trail for every decision.
- **Moneypenny**: Hybrid escalation model — AI handles routine calls, transfers to human receptionists for complex situations. "Pledge People, Not Bots" is a different competitor's slogan (AnswerConnect); Moneypenny explicitly combines both.

### Data processing
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch draft processing stance. Website host: DigitalOcean. Planned product subprocessors (not live claims on this static site): Cloudflare (CDN/WAF), Twilio (voice), Google (Gemini/Piper), SendGrid (email). Draft register at /subprocessors/ — pending legal review.
- **Moneypenny**: Homepage JSON-LD lists technology partners: Twilio, Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Deepgram, MongoDB. Privacy policy at moneypenny.com/privacy-policy. UK and US operations.

### Pricing
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch — no public prices. Early access packages shared on request via /demo/.
- **Moneypenny**: Quote-only for both Phone Answering and AI Receptionist plans. "Get in touch" forms lead to sales consultation (source: moneypenny.com/us/phone-answering-service and moneypenny.com/us/ai-receptionist, accessed 2026-10-19).

### UK-specific features
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Companies House lookup, UK postcode validation, UK regulations awareness (gas safety, electrical, etc.), UK voice demo number (when enabled).
- **Moneypenny**: UK entity with UK team. UK phone numbers available. UK-specific industry pages (legal, healthcare, finance, real estate, home services, franchise) at moneypenny.com/uk.

### Availability
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch. Website, scanner, and voice demo are functional previews. No paid plans available for purchase.
- **Moneypenny**: Generally available since 2000. 25 years in business. 10,000s of businesses onboard (source: moneypenny.com homepage JSON-LD, accessed 2026-10-19).
