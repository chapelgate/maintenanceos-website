## Detailed comparison

### Product category
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch UK maintenance operations software with AI-assisted intake. The AI receptionist capability is under testing. Fixed rules and authorised human clearance keep control of safety and money. Not commercially generally available; not a public always-on phone service.
- **Rosie**: "The easiest AI answering service for small business. Rosie is trained on your business and works 24/7 — answering questions, booking appointments, and texting you the details of every call. Starting at $49/month" (source: heyrosie.com homepage, accessed 2026-10-19). 2,000+ small businesses. 3.1M+ calls handled, 3.3M minutes saved, 35k appointment links sent, 210k texts sent (source: heyrosie.com homepage stats, accessed 2026-10-19).

### AI capability
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: AI extracts structured repair facts from natural language (website scanner + voice). Safety latches hold dangerous/high-value/high-risk items for authorised human decision. Voice gate: low-confidence fields reported as "Unknown — not found".
- **Rosie**: Auto-trained from website and Google Business Profile. Custom FAQs. 10+ voices. Custom agent name/greeting. English & Spanish on every call. Books appointments directly to calendar or sends scheduling links. Warm/cold transfers. Website Chat included free on all plans. Website Texting add-on ($50/mo). Zapier integration (1,000+ apps). iOS/Android app for managing calls.

### Safety architecture
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Draft present-fact capability. Safety latches documented in product source; audit trail for every decision documented in source only.
- **Rosie**: "Smart Message Taking asks different questions depending on why they called. Filters out spam and sales calls automatically. Books appointments directly into your calendar or texts a scheduling link. Transfers calls to you or your team, warm or cold. Every call logged with AI-generated summary and full transcript and recording" (source: heyrosie.com homepage, accessed 2026-10-19). Premium plans add "waterfall transfers" (tries multiple numbers) and custom agent training.

### Data processing
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch draft processing stance. Website host: DigitalOcean. Planned product subprocessors (not live claims on this static site): Cloudflare (CDN/WAF), Twilio (voice), Google (Gemini/Piper), SendGrid (email). Draft register at /subprocessors/ — pending legal review.
- **Rosie**: US-based. Uses Twilio for voice (implied by phone number handling). Zapier integration for 1,000+ apps. Privacy policy at heyrosie.com/legal/privacy. Terms at heyrosie.com/legal/terms. No public subprocessor list found.

### Pricing
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch — no public prices. Early access packages shared on request via /demo/.
- **Rosie**: Public transparent pricing (source: heyrosie.com/pricing, accessed 2026-10-19):
  - Professional: $49/mo — 250 minutes, 2 custom message scenarios, appointment links by text, spam detection, website chat
  - Scale (Most Popular): $149/mo — 1,000 minutes, 5 scenarios, direct calendar booking, warm transfers, live call transfers, send texts during calls
  - Growth: $299/mo — 2,000 minutes, unlimited scenarios, waterfall transfers, custom agent training (upload docs), white-glove onboarding, priority support
  - All plans: 7-day free trial, cancel anytime, iOS/Android apps, 24/7, multiple simultaneous calls, 10+ voices, EN/ES, custom area code, summaries/transcripts/recordings, email/text notifications, Zapier
  - Add-ons: Website Chat (free), Website Texting ($50/mo, 25 convos then $1/convo)

### UK-specific features
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch planned: Companies House lookup, UK postcode validation, UK regulations awareness (gas safety, electrical, etc.), UK voice demo number (when enabled) — not live on this static site.
- **Rosie**: US-focused. US phone numbers (pick area code). English & Spanish. No UK Companies House, UK postcode validation, or UK regulation awareness mentioned. UK businesses would need US number or porting.

### Availability
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch. Website, scanner, and voice demo are functional previews. No paid plans available for purchase.
- **Rosie**: Generally available. 7-day free trial. 2,000+ businesses. Industries: Home Services, Law Firms, Property Management, Construction, Real Estate, Plumbing, Automotive, Salons, and "other small businesses" (source: heyrosie.com/industries, accessed 2026-10-19). Comparison blogs: Rosie vs Smith.ai, Rosie vs Goodcall (source: heyrosie.com/blog).
