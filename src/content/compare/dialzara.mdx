## Detailed comparison

### Product category
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch UK maintenance operations software with AI-assisted intake. The AI receptionist capability is under testing. Fixed rules and authorised human clearance keep control of safety and money. Not commercially generally available; not a public always-on phone service.
- **Dialzara**: "AI-powered virtual receptionist and phone answering service for small businesses. Never miss a call again with 24/7 AI phone agents" (source: dialzara.com homepage JSON-LD, accessed 2026-10-19). Founded 2023. Based in Eagle, Idaho, USA. Single platform: inbound AI receptionist, outbound voice agents, two-way AI SMS agents, embeddable website chatbot — all sharing a knowledge base.

### AI capability
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: AI extracts structured repair facts from natural language (website scanner + voice). Safety latches hold dangerous/high-value/high-risk items for authorised human decision. Voice gate: low-confidence fields reported as "Unknown — not found".
- **Dialzara**: Inbound AI receptionist answers calls 24/7, books appointments to calendar, sends detailed call summaries via email, screens spam, qualifies leads. Outbound voice agents for sales outreach, follow-ups, reminders, reactivation (separate plans from $750/mo). AI SMS agents with 10DLC A2P registration ($19/mo + $0.05/AI message). Website chatbot ($39/mo standalone or free with voice plan). 50+ voices. Knowledge base from website/docs/recordings.

### Safety architecture
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Draft present-fact capability. Safety latches documented in product source; audit trail for every decision documented in source only.
- **Dialzara**: "Can intelligently route complex calls to you or your team when needed. You can also customize responses for any scenario and set up escalation rules. Multiple transfer extensions supported for routing different call types" (source: dialzara.com FAQ, accessed 2026-10-19). No published "safety latch" or fixed-rule gating.

### Data processing
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: UK-only processing. Subprocessors: DigitalOcean (hosting), Cloudflare (CDN/WAF), Twilio (voice), Google (Gemini/Piper), SendGrid (email). Published at /subprocessors/.
- **Dialzara**: US-based (Dialzara LLC, 408 S Eagle Rd Suite 53, Eagle, ID 83616). Uses Twilio for voice. Privacy policy at dialzara.com/privacy (implied). No public subprocessor list found. Homepage JSON-LD lists contactPoint with US areaServed.

### Pricing
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch — no public prices. Early access packages shared on request via /demo/.
- **Dialzara**: Public transparent pricing (source: dialzara.com homepage JSON-LD SoftwareApplication offers, accessed 2026-10-19):
  - Inbound Business Lite: $29/mo
  - Inbound Business Pro: $99/mo
  - Inbound Business Plus: $199/mo
  - Inbound Business Elite: $349/mo
  - Outbound Voice Starter: $750/mo (1,500 contacts)
  - Outbound Voice Growth: $1,000/mo
  - Outbound Voice Scale: $1,500/mo
  - Website Chatbot Standalone: $39/mo
  - AI SMS Agent: $19/mo + $0.05 per AI message
  - 7-day free trial, no setup fees, cancel anytime (source: dialzara.com FAQ)

### UK-specific features
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch planned: Companies House lookup, UK postcode validation, UK regulations awareness (gas safety, electrical, etc.), UK voice demo number (when enabled) — not live on this static site.
- **Dialzara**: US-focused. US phone numbers (local/toll-free). No UK Companies House, UK postcode validation, or UK regulation awareness mentioned. UK businesses would need US number or porting.

### Availability
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch. Website, scanner, and voice demo are functional previews. No paid plans available for purchase.
- **Dialzara**: Generally available. Founded 2023. Live demo number: 866-545-ZARA (866-545-9272). Serves 60+ industries (source: dialzara.com/industries, accessed 2026-10-19). Comparison pages vs Goodcall, Smith.ai, Ruby, RingCentral, Allo, Quo, JustCall (source: dialzara.com/compare).
