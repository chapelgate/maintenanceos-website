## Detailed comparison

### Product category
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch UK maintenance operations software with AI-assisted intake. The AI receptionist capability is under testing. Fixed rules and authorised human clearance keep control of safety and money. Not commercially generally available; not a public always-on phone service.
- **Phena**: "The AI receptionist for UK small businesses. Phena picks up when you can't. It answers questions, books jobs into your diary and passes anything tricky to you, day and night. No menus, no voicemail, no lost work" (source: getphena.com homepage, accessed 2026-10-19). UK company: Code Garden Services UK Ltd, Company No. 14463471 (source: getphena.com footer, accessed 2026-10-19). 14-day free trial, setup in 2 minutes.

### AI capability
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: AI extracts structured repair facts from natural language (website scanner + voice). Safety latches hold dangerous/high-value/high-risk items for authorised human decision. Voice gate: low-confidence fields reported as "Unknown — not found".
- **Phena**: "Answers questions from your FAQ. Books appointments in real time. Smart handoff with full context. 24/7 always answering. <2s pick-up time. 0 made-up answers. 2 min typical setup time" (source: getphena.com homepage). "Phena only says what you've told it. If it doesn't know something, it says so and offers to take a message or put the caller through to you. It never guesses" (source: getphena.com FAQ). Pre-configured templates for 17 industries (plumbers, electricians, restaurants, salons, dentists, auto shops, contractors, cleaners, HVAC, landscapers, vets, fitness studios, spas, real estate, locksmiths, handymen, heating engineers, garages, estate agents, tattoo studios). Supports US, UK, France, Germany with locale-aware voice, language, call-forwarding codes.

### Safety architecture
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Draft present-fact capability. Safety latches documented in product source; audit trail for every decision documented in source only.
- **Phena**: Zero hallucination architecture — "only states facts from your own setup. If information is missing or ambiguous, it transfers to you rather than guess. Every call is recorded and transcribed for review" (source: getphena.com FAQ). Smart handoff: "Anything unusual comes to you with a summary and the caller's details. Nothing important slips past" (source: getphena.com homepage). ROI calculator shows missed call recovery.

### Data processing
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch draft processing stance. Website host: DigitalOcean. Planned product subprocessors (not live claims on this static site): Cloudflare (CDN/WAF), Twilio (voice), Google (Gemini/Piper), SendGrid (email). Draft register at /subprocessors/ — pending legal review.
- **Phena**: UK company (Code Garden Services UK Ltd). Uses Twilio for voice — "UK businesses need a Twilio regulatory bundle (24-hour approval). French mobile numbers can't be used for AI agents under ARCEP rules, so use a local or toll-free number there. German businesses need a commercial register extract" (source: getphena.com FAQ). Privacy policy at getphena.com/legal/privacy. Terms at getphena.com/legal/terms. DPA at getphena.com/legal/dpa. AUP at getphena.com/legal/aup. No public subprocessor list beyond Twilio.

### Pricing
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch — no public prices. Early access packages shared on request via /demo/.
- **Phena**: Public transparent pricing (source: getphena.com pricing section, accessed 2026-10-19):
  - Starter: £29/mo — 1 location, 100 call minutes/mo, 50 bookings/mo, email notifications, unlimited FAQs
  - Professional (Most Popular): £79/mo — up to 3 locations, 400 minutes/mo, 200 bookings/mo, SMS+email alerts, voice cloning, call analytics, API access
  - Scale: £119/mo — up to 10 locations, 900 minutes/mo, 1,000 bookings/mo, voice cloning, priority support, advanced analytics, dedicated onboarding
  - Enterprise: Custom — unlimited businesses/locations, franchise support, custom workflows, on-prem/private cloud, SSO/SAML, white-label, custom SLA, dedicated account manager
  - All plans: zero-hallucination AI, full call transcripts, human handover, 14-day free trial, no contracts, cancel anytime

- **Phena**: UK company (Company No. 14463471). UK Twilio regulatory bundle (24-hour approval). Supports UK, US, France, Germany with locale-aware voice/language/codes. French ARCEP compliance (no mobile numbers for AI). German commercial register extract requirement. 17 UK-focused industry templates.

### Availability

- **Phena**: Generally available. Live demo call available (no signup). "All systems operational" status. 14-day free trial, no credit card, cancel anytime. Industries: plumbers, builders/contractors, dental, cleaning, hair salons, solicitors, electricians, handymen, vets, landscapers, fitness studios, tattoo studios, heating engineers, garages, estate agents, restaurants, spas (source: getphena.com footer industries, accessed 2026-10-19).
