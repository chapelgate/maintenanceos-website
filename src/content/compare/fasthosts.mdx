## Detailed comparison

### Product category
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch UK maintenance operations software with AI-assisted intake. The AI receptionist capability is under testing. Fixed rules and authorised human clearance keep control of safety and money. Not commercially generally available; not a public always-on phone service.
- **Fasthosts**: UK web hosting provider since 1998. Offers domains, web hosting, VPS, dedicated servers, email, website builder, and AI Receptionist as an add-on product. Over 1 million domains, thousands of servers (source: fasthosts.co.uk homepage, accessed 2026-10-19).

### AI capability
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: AI extracts structured repair facts from natural language (website scanner + voice). Safety latches hold dangerous/high-value/high-risk items for authorised human decision. Voice gate: low-confidence fields reported as "Unknown — not found".
- **Fasthosts AI Receptionist**: "Never miss a business call, even after hours. Captures every lead – even when you're off the clock. Automatically books appointments right on the phone. Sends instant call summaries to your inbox. Cancel online at any time. 30+ languages. Calendly & CRM integrations" (source: fasthosts.co.uk/spark/ai-receptionist, accessed 2026-10-19). Marketed as part of "Spark" product line.

### Safety architecture
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Explicit safety latches (dangerous work, high value, gas/electrical, safeguarding). Human clearance required. Audit trail for every decision.
- **Fasthosts**: No published safety latch architecture. AI Receptionist features focus on lead capture and appointment booking. Unclear how hazardous/emergency situations are handled.

### Data processing
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch draft processing stance. Website host: DigitalOcean. Planned product subprocessors (not live claims on this static site): Cloudflare (CDN/WAF), Twilio (voice), Google (Gemini/Piper), SendGrid (email). Draft register at /subprocessors/ — pending legal review.
- **Fasthosts**: UK data centres (Worcester). ISO 27001-certified, Tier IV-certified (99.999% availability), 100% renewable electricity, HVO biofuel generators, ISO 50001 energy management, solar panels on Worcester data centre roof (source: fasthosts.co.uk/about/data-centres, accessed 2026-10-19). "Proudly British — data kept locally". Privacy notice at fasthosts.co.uk/terms/policies/privacy-notice.

### Pricing
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch — no public prices. Early access packages shared on request via /demo/.
- **Fasthosts**: AI Receptionist from £39/month (source: fasthosts.co.uk/spark/ai-receptionist and homepage promo, accessed 2026-10-19). Hosting from 99p/month, domains from £1/year. Clear-cut pricing claimed: "some of the most stable prices out there" (source: fasthosts.co.uk homepage).

### UK-specific features
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Companies House lookup, UK postcode validation, UK regulations awareness (gas safety, electrical, etc.), UK voice demo number (when enabled).
- **Fasthosts**: UK-based since 1998 (Gloucester HQ). UK data centres. UK data sovereignty. British business. Medium Business of the Year 2025 (SoGlos). Green hosting (100% renewable).

### Availability
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch. Website, scanner, and voice demo are functional previews. No paid plans available for purchase.
- **Fasthosts**: Generally available since 1998. 25+ years experience. Trustpilot 4.8/5 from 22,177 reviews (source: fasthosts.co.uk homepage, accessed 2026-10-19).
