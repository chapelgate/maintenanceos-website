## Detailed comparison

### Product category
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch UK maintenance operations software with AI-assisted intake. The AI receptionist capability is under testing. Fixed rules and authorised human clearance keep control of safety and money. Not commercially generally available; not a public always-on phone service.
- **Fixflo**: Established repairs and maintenance management software (SaaS) for lettings, block management, commercial (CAFM), build-to-rent, social housing, student accommodation, and tradespeople. Part of Aareon. Over 2,000 agencies, 1.6M+ units, 150k+ repairs/month (source: fixflo.com homepage, accessed 2026-10-19).

### AI capability
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: AI extracts structured repair facts from natural language (website scanner + voice). Safety latches hold dangerous/high-value/high-risk items for authorised human decision. Voice gate: low-confidence fields reported as "Unknown — not found".
- **Fixflo (Aidenn AI)**: Two AI products marketed: (1) Aidenn AI repairs & maintenance — "AI repairs assistant fully integrated with Fixflo" (source: fixflo.com/solutions/aidenn-ai-repairs-maintenance); (2) Aidenn AI compliance — "process complex Fire Risk Assessments in minutes" (source: fixflo.com/aidenn-ai-compliance). No public detail on safety architecture or human-in-the-loop gating.

### Safety architecture
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Draft present-fact capability. Safety latches documented in product source; audit trail for every decision documented in source only.
- **Fixflo**: Workflow management and compliance features; contractor marketplace. No published "safety latch" or human-gated AI architecture.

### Data processing
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch draft processing stance. Website host: DigitalOcean. Planned product subprocessors (not live claims on this static site): Cloudflare (CDN/WAF), Twilio (voice), Google (Gemini/Piper), SendGrid (email). Draft register at /subprocessors/ — pending legal review.
- **Fixflo**: UK-hosted (Aareon). Privacy policy at fixflo.com/privacy-policy. No public subprocessor list found.

### Pricing
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch — no public prices. Early access packages shared on request via /demo/.
- **Fixflo**: Quote-only pricing per segment (lettings, block management, build-to-rent, commercial). No public list prices (source: fixflo.com/pricing/lettings, accessed 2026-10-19).

### UK-specific features
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch planned: Companies House lookup, UK postcode validation, UK regulations awareness (gas safety, electrical, etc.), UK voice demo number (when enabled) — not live on this static site.
- **Fixflo**: UK-focused product for UK property sectors (lettings, block management, social housing, BTR, student accommodation). Section 20 compliance for block management. ANUK/Unipol Code for student accommodation.

### Availability
- **WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS**: Pre-launch. Website, scanner, and voice demo are functional previews. No paid plans available for purchase.
- **Fixflo**: Generally available. 2,000+ agencies, 120k+ users (source: fixflo.com homepage, accessed 2026-10-19).
