# UK Maintenance Regulations: What Property Teams Need to Know

UK property maintenance isn't just about fixing things — it's a regulated activity with criminal liability for non-compliance. This overview covers the core obligations for landlords, letting agents, block managers, and housing providers. It is not legal advice.

## Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998

**Who it applies to**: Any landlord or letting agent with gas appliances in rented properties.

**Core obligations**:
- Annual gas safety check by Gas Safe registered engineer
- Gas Safety Record (CP12) provided to tenants within 28 days
- Record retained for 2 years
- Only Gas Safe engineers may work on gas appliances

**Penalties**: Unlimited fine, up to 6 months imprisonment, or both (Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974, s.33).

**Source**: HSE — Gas safety for landlords (https://www.hse.gov.uk/gas/landlords.htm, accessed 2026-10-19)

## Electrical Safety Standards in the Private Rented Sector (England) Regulations 2020

**Who it applies to**: Private landlords in England (similar rules in Scotland/Wales).

**Core obligations**:
- Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR) every 5 years by qualified electrician
- Report provided to tenants within 28 days
- Remedial work completed within 28 days (or sooner if report specifies)
- Written confirmation of remedial work to tenants and local authority

**Penalties**: Financial penalty up to £30,000 per breach (local authority enforcement).

**Source**: UK Government — Electrical safety standards in the private rented sector (https://www.gov.uk/guidance/electrical-safety-standards-in-the-private-rented-sector, accessed 2026-10-19)

## Fire Safety Order 2005 (Regulatory Reform)

**Who it applies to**: "Responsible persons" for non-domestic premises — including common parts of blocks of flats, HMOs, commercial properties.

**Core obligations**:
- Fire Risk Assessment (FRA) by competent person
- FRA reviewed regularly and after significant changes
- General fire precautions maintained
- Records kept

**Penalties**: Unlimited fine, up to 2 years imprisonment.

**Source**: UK Government — Fire safety in the workplace (https://www.gov.uk/workplace-fire-safety-your-responsibilities, accessed 2026-10-19)

## Section 20 (Landlord and Tenant Act 1985)

**Who it applies to**: Landlords of leasehold flats where service charges exceed £250 per leaseholder for qualifying works, or £100 per year for long-term agreements.

**Core obligations**:
- Stage 1: Notice of intention (description, reasons, estimated cost)
- Stage 2: Notice of estimates (at least 2 estimates, 30 days to observe)
- Stage 3: Notice of reasons (if not accepting lowest estimate)
- Leaseholders can nominate contractors

**Penalties**: Service charge limited to £250/£100 if consultation not followed; leaseholder may challenge at Tribunal.

**Source**: Leasehold Advisory Service — Section 20 consultation (https://www.lease-advice.org/advice-guide/section-20-consultation/, accessed 2026-10-19)

## Safeguarding (Care Act 2014, Children Act 1989/2004)

**Who it applies to**: Housing providers, councils, contractors entering homes where vulnerable adults or children may be present.

**Core obligations**:
- DBS checks for staff/contractors in regulated activity
- Safeguarding policies and training
- Reporting concerns to local authority
- Multi-agency cooperation
