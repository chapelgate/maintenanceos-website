# Structured Repair Intake vs Free-Text Forms: What Changes for UK Teams

Most UK property teams still collect maintenance requests through free-text forms, email, or phone messages. The tenant writes what they think is relevant; the coordinator interprets; the contractor arrives with incomplete information. This cycle repeats — creating rework, missed safety issues, and no audit trail.

## The Free-Text Problem

**Typical free-text request**: "Boiler not working properly, house is cold"

**What the coordinator doesn't know**:
- Is it gas, oil, or electric?
- Is there a carbon monoxide risk?
- Is the tenant vulnerable (elderly, young children)?
- Is this a total loss of heating (emergency) or partial?
- What's the boiler make/model/age?
- Has the tenant tried reset/basic checks?

**Result**: Multiple follow-up calls, wrong contractor dispatched, safety risk missed, no evidence for compliance.

## Structured AI-Assisted Intake

MaintenanceOS uses AI to extract structured facts from natural language — whether typed on a website or spoken to the voice demo. The AI asks follow-up questions based on fixed rules:

1. **Asset identification**: Boiler make/model, location, age
2. **Hazard classification**: Gas/electrical/water/safeguarding flags
3. **Urgency grading**: Total loss, partial, inconvenience — mapped to response SLAs
4. **Tenant context**: Vulnerability indicators, access arrangements
5. **History check**: Previous reports on same asset/address

The output is a structured repair draft — not a classification label, but a complete fact set ready for human review.

## Safety Latches Apply Automatically

The structured data triggers safety latches without coordinator judgement:

- Gas appliance mentioned → holds for Gas Safe engineer clearance
- Electrical hazard keywords → holds for NICEIC clearance
- Vulnerable tenant flagged → escalates to safeguarding lead
- Value threshold exceeded → requires authorised sign-off

Every latch decision is recorded: who cleared, when, what evidence.

## Audit Evidence by Default

Free-text forms create no evidence. Structured intake creates:

- Timestamped fact extraction log
- Safety latch trigger and clearance records
- Contractor brief with complete structured data
- Tenant communication trail
- Compliance export for regulators
