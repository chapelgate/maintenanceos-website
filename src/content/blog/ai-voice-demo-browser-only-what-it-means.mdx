# AI Voice Demo: Browser-Only — What It Means

The WorkSmartOS voice demo runs in your browser. No phone number. No app install. No signup. This is intentional — it's a functional preview of the AI receptionist capability under testing, not a live phone service.

## How the Browser Demo Works

1. **Click "Try Voice Demo"** on /create/ or /demo/
2. **Grant microphone permission** (browser prompt)
3. **Speak naturally** — describe a maintenance issue
4. **AI extracts structured facts** in real time
5. **Review the draft** — see what the AI heard and structured
6. **Safety latches apply** — gas, electrical, safeguarding, high-value flags appear

The demo uses Google Gemini for language understanding and Piper for text-to-speech, running client-side where possible and via our API for the voice components.

## What It Is Not

- ❌ Not a live phone number you can call
- ❌ Not an always-on receptionist service
- ❌ Not general availability
- ❌ Not a substitute for emergency services (999/111)
- ❌ Not a contracted service — no SLA, no support

## What It Is

- ✅ Functional preview of structured fact extraction from speech
- ✅ Safety latch demonstration (gas/electrical/safeguarding/high-value)
- ✅ Browser-based — works on desktop and mobile
- ✅ No data retained after session ends
- ✅ Honest about pre-launch status

## Preview Demo Number (Under Testing)

We are testing a Twilio-powered preview demo number that routes to the same voice pipeline. When the preview gate is enabled, it may appear on /create/ and /demo/ pages. This is separate from the browser demo and also pre-launch. Not a live always-on phone.

- **Availability**: only when the preview gate is enabled — not a public 24/7 answering service
- **Scope**: Greeting + structured intake only
- **Safety**: Same safety latches apply
- **Data**: Call audio processed per our subprocessors list (/subprocessors/)
- **Cost**: Free during pre-launch testing

The preview number is gated behind the same voice gate enforcement (S7 in our master plan) — low-confidence fields reported as "Unknown — not found", not guessed.

## Why Browser-First?

1. **Accessibility**: No phone required; works for deaf/hard-of-hearing users with text fallback
2. **Privacy**: No phone number shared; microphone permission is explicit and revocable
3. **Testing**: Faster iteration on the AI pipeline without telephony infrastructure
4. **Transparency**: You see exactly what the AI extracts in real time

## Pre-Launch Status

WorkSmartOS MaintenanceOS is pre-launch. The AI receptionist capability is under testing. Fixed rules and authorised human clearance keep control of safety and money. Not commercially generally available; not a public always-on phone service.

Early access packages shared on request via /demo/.
