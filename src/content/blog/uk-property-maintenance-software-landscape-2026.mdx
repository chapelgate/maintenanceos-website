# UK Property Maintenance Software Landscape 2026

The UK property maintenance software market has consolidated around a few established platforms, while a new wave of AI-assisted tools enters pre-launch. This overview cites only public sources accessed in October 2026.

## Established Platforms

### Fixflo (Aareon)
- **Category**: Repairs and maintenance management SaaS
- **Segments**: Lettings, block management, commercial (CAFM), build-to-rent, social housing, student accommodation
- **Scale**: 2,000+ agencies, 1.6M+ units, 150k+ repairs/month (source: fixflo.com homepage, accessed 2026-10-19)
- **AI**: Aidenn AI repairs assistant + Aidenn AI compliance for Fire Risk Assessments (source: fixflo.com/solutions/aidenn-ai-repairs-maintenance, accessed 2026-10-19)
- **Pricing**: Quote-only per segment (source: fixflo.com/pricing/lettings, accessed 2026-10-19)
- **Data**: UK-hosted (Aareon); privacy policy at fixflo.com/privacy-policy

### Arthur Online
- **Category**: Property management platform with maintenance module
- **Segments**: Lettings, block management, portfolio management
- **Scale**: 10,000+ properties managed (source: arthuronline.co.uk, accessed 2026-10-19)
- **AI**: No public AI maintenance intake product
- **Pricing**: Per-unit monthly from £1.50 (source: arthuronline.co.uk/pricing, accessed 2026-10-19)

### Mede (formerly Qube)
- **Category**: Block management and maintenance software
- **Segments**: Residential block management, estate management
- **AI**: No public AI maintenance intake product
- **Pricing**: Quote-based
