# Getting Started with MaintenanceOS

Welcome to MaintenanceOS. This guide walks you through the initial setup so your team can start managing maintenance operations safely and efficiently.

## 1. Create Your Workspace

After accepting your invitation, you'll be prompted to create a workspace. A workspace is your organisation's home in MaintenanceOS — it holds your properties, team members, and maintenance history.

- Choose a workspace name (usually your company or portfolio name).
- Select your primary currency (GBP for UK teams).
- Confirm your time zone (defaults to Europe/London).

## 2. Add Properties

Properties are the buildings or units you maintain. You can add them individually or import a CSV.

**Required fields per property:**
- Address (UK postcode preferred for auto-complete)
- Property type (residential, commercial, mixed-use)
- Number of units
- Primary contact (name, phone, email)

**Optional but recommended:**
- Gas safety certificate expiry
- EPC rating
- HMO licence number (if applicable)
- Access instructions for contractors

## 3. Invite Your Team

MaintenanceOS supports role-based access:

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Admin** | Full access: properties, team, billing, integrations |
| **Manager** | Properties, repairs, contractors, reports |
| **Contractor** | Assigned repairs only, update status, upload photos |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to repairs and reports |

Invite team members by email. They'll receive a secure link to join.

## 4. Configure Repair Categories

Customise repair categories to match your workflow. Default categories include:
- Plumbing
- Electrical
- Heating & Hot Water
- Structural
- Damp & Mould
- Gas Safety
- Common Areas
- External & Grounds

You can add, rename, or archive categories at any time.

## 5. Set Up Safety Rules

MaintenanceOS uses **fixed safety rules** to flag high-risk repairs. Review the default rules and adjust thresholds if needed:

- **Gas smell / carbon monoxide** → Immediate escalation, 24/7
- **No heating in winter (Oct–Mar)** → 4-hour SLA
- **Water leak affecting electrics** → 2-hour SLA
- **Structural instability** → Immediate escalation
- **Fire safety equipment fault** → 24-hour SLA

These rules cannot be disabled — they ensure compliance with UK regulations.

## 6. Connect Your Channels (Optional)

MaintenanceOS can receive repair reports from:
- **Website widget** (embed on your tenant portal)
- **Email forwarding** (dedicated address per workspace)
- **Phone** (via Twilio — requires admin setup)
- **API** (for custom integrations)

You can enable channels one at a time. Start with the website widget for the fastest setup.

## 7. Run a Test Repair

Before going live, submit a test repair through your chosen channel. Verify:
- The repair appears in the dashboard
- Correct category and priority are assigned
- Safety rules trigger as expected
- Notifications reach the right people
