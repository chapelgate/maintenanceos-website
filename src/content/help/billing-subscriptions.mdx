# Billing & Subscriptions

MaintenanceOS is pre-launch. There is no published price list and no self-serve checkout on this site. This page describes how billing is expected to work after early access opens; it is not a live offer.

## Pricing Plans (Pre-Launch)

List packages are not published here. Request pricing and we will share what fits your portfolio. Planned workspace shapes (names only — not prices):

| Plan | Includes (illustrative) |
|------|-------------------------|
| **Starter** | Up to 50 properties, 5 team members, core repairs, basic compliance, email support |
| **Professional** | Up to 500 properties, 25 team members, all compliance, contractor portal, API access, Slack/Teams, priority support |
| **Enterprise** | Unlimited properties, SSO, dedicated SLAs, custom integrations, on-premise option, account manager |

Early-access terms are shared on request. No percentage discount is published on this page.

## What Counts as a Property

- Each unique address (building or unit)
- HMOs: Each licensed HMO = 1 property (flats within not counted separately)
- Blocks: Each block = 1 property (flats within not counted separately)
- Commercial units: Each unit = 1 property

## Billing Cycle

- **Monthly:** Charged 1st of each month, in advance
- **Annual:** Charged on anniversary, in advance
- **Proration:** Upgrades immediate, downgrades at cycle end
- **Currency:** GBP only (UK entities)

## Payment Methods

- **Card** (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) — Stripe processed
- **Direct Debit** (Bacs) — GoCardless, UK bank accounts only
- **Invoice** (Enterprise only) — Net 30 terms, PO required

**Failed payments:** 3 retry attempts over 7 days → Workspace suspended → Data retained 90 days → Then deleted.

## Managing Your Subscription

**Settings → Billing → Subscription:**

- View current plan, next billing date, usage vs limits
- **Upgrade:** Immediate, prorated charge
- **Downgrade:** Takes effect next cycle, confirm data fits new limits
- **Cancel:** Access continues until period end, then workspace read-only 30 days, then deleted

## Invoices

**Settings → Billing → Invoices:**
- All historical invoices (PDF download)
- VAT breakdown (UK VAT 20%)
- Invoice number format: `MOS-{YYYY}-{NNNNN}`
- Auto-emailed to billing contact

**VAT:** VAT treatment is confirmed in the agreement you are offered. This page does not publish a VAT number.

## Team Members & Seats

- Each plan includes a seat allowance
- Extra seat rates are shared on request — not listed here
- Remove member → Seat frees immediately
- Invite pending → Counts toward limit

## Usage Alerts

- **80% of property limit:** Email to admins
- **95% of property limit:** Email + in-app banner
- **100%:** New properties blocked until upgrade or removal

## Data Portability

**Export anytime:** Settings → Billing → Export Data
- Properties, repairs, contractors, compliance (CSV + JSON)
- API bulk export for Enterprise

**On cancellation:** 30-day read-only access + full export available.

## Enterprise Features

- **SSO/SAML** (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace)
- **Custom contracts** (MSA, DPA, Security Questionnaire)
- **Dedicated infrastructure** (optional)
- **Uptime and support targets** are not published. Not a live SLA. They will be in the agreement you are offered.
- **Data residency** (UK-only hosting available)
- **Penetration test reports** (annual)
