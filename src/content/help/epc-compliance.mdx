# EPC Compliance & MEES

Energy Performance Certificates (EPCs) rate property energy efficiency from A (best) to G (worst). UK rental properties must meet Minimum Energy Efficiency Standards (MEES).

## Current MEES Requirements

| Property Type | Minimum Rating | Effective |
|---------------|----------------|-----------|
| **Domestic new lets** | E | 1 Apr 2018 |
| **Domestic existing tenancies** | E | 1 Apr 2020 |
| **Non-domestic new lets** | E | 1 Apr 2018 |
| **Non-domestic existing tenancies** | E | 1 Apr 2023 |

**Proposed changes (consultation stage):**
- Domestic: **C by 2028** (new lets), **C by 2030** (all)
- Non-domestic: **B by 2030**

## EPC Tracking in MaintenanceOS

Each property → EPC tab:
- Current rating + certificate number + expiry date
- **Auto-warning:** 6 months before expiry (EPCs valid 10 years)
- **MEES status:** Compliant / Exempt / Non-compliant
- Improvement recommendations from certificate

## Exemptions

Valid exemptions (must be registered on PRS Exemptions Register):
- **High cost:** Improvements exceed £3,500 cap (domestic)
- **7-year payback:** Improvements don't pay back in 7 years
- **Wall insulation:** Expert confirms not feasible
- **Consent:** Planning / listed building / tenant refusal
- **Devaluation:** Improvements reduce value >5%

MaintenanceOS tracks:
- Exemption type
- Registration date + expiry (5 years)
- Supporting evidence (quotes, expert reports)
- Auto-warning before exemption expiry

## Improvement Planning

When EPC < E (or targeting C for future compliance):

1. Property → EPC → **View Recommendations**
2. See prioritised list: measure, indicative cost, rating impact
3. **Create Improvement Plan:**
   - Select measures
   - Set budget + timeline
   - Assign to contractor / project manager
   - Track progress

**Example path to C:**
| Measure | Cost | Rating Gain |
|---------|------|-------------|
| Loft insulation (270mm) | £300–£500 | +5–10 pts |
| Cavity wall insulation | £500–£1,500 | +5–10 pts |
| Double glazing | £3,000–£6,000 | +5–10 pts |
| Air source heat pump | £7,000–£13,000 | +15–25 pts |
| Solar PV | £4,000–£8,000 | +5–10 pts |

## Funding & Grants

MaintenanceOS flags applicable schemes:
- **ECO4** (Energy Company Obligation) — low-income tenants
- **Great British Insulation Scheme** — cavity/loft
- **Boiler Upgrade Scheme** — heat pumps (£7,500)
- **Local authority grants** — area-specific

Link at Property → EPC → **Available Funding**.

## Non-Domestic EPCs

For commercial properties:
- Separate certificate (valid 10 years)
- Display certificate in building (>500m², public access)
- Recommendation report (valid 7 years)
- MEES: Minimum E, targeting B by 2030

## Reporting

**Reports → EPC Compliance:**
- Portfolio rating distribution
- Expiring soon (6 months)
- Non-compliant + exemption status
- Improvement plan progress
- Cost estimates for portfolio upgrade to C
