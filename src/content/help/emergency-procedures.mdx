# Emergency Procedures

MaintenanceOS is designed to handle emergencies safely and compliantly. This guide covers the critical procedures every team member should know.

## Emergency Contact Numbers

**Always call 999 first for immediate danger to life.**

| Emergency | Number | When to Call |
|-----------|--------|--------------|
| **Gas leak / carbon monoxide** | 0800 111 999 (National Gas Emergency) | Smell gas, CO alarm sounding |
| **Fire** | 999 | Any fire, smoke, burning smell |
| **Electrical emergency** | 105 (Power Cut) / 999 if danger | Exposed wires, sparking, shock risk |
| **Water main burst** | Local water company emergency | Uncontrolled flooding |
| **Structural collapse risk** | 999 | Building movement, major cracks |
| **Asbestos disturbance** | 999 + HSE 0300 003 1647 | Suspected asbestos release |

## MaintenanceOS Emergency Flow

### 1. Emergency Repair Reported
- Tenant/contractor reports via portal, phone, or email
- **Safety keywords** (gas, fire, flood, collapse, electrical) → **Auto-triggers Safety Latch**
- Repair status: **Safety Hold** (cannot be assigned)
- **Immediate notifications:** All Admins + Safety Officer (email + SMS + Slack/Teams)

### 2. Safety Officer Review (Target: < 30 minutes)
1. Open repair → **Safety Hold** banner → "Review"
2. Assess: Is 999 already called? Is property evacuated?
3. If not already done → **Call appropriate emergency service**
4. Select qualified emergency contractor (Gas Safe, NICEIC, etc.)
5. Add mandatory clearance note
6. **Clear Hold** → Repair becomes **Emergency** priority

### 3. Emergency Assignment
- Only contractors with **emergency availability** and **correct certifications** shown
- Contractor must **acknowledge within 30 minutes**
- SLA: **On site within 2–4 hours** (gas: 1 hour)
- Contractor receives: Property access, tenant contact, safety notes

### 4. On Site & Resolution
- Contractor updates: En Route → On Site → Completed
- **Photos mandatory** for emergency repairs
- If further danger found → New Safety Latch triggered
- Completion notes must include: Cause, fix, certification references

### 5. Post-Emergency
- Safety Officer reviews within 24 hours
- Tenant welfare check (automated prompt)
- Compliance certificates updated (Gas Safe, EICR, etc.)
- Incident logged in **Reports → Safety Incidents**

## Specific Emergency Protocols

### Gas Leak / Carbon Monoxide
1. **Tenant instruction (auto-sent):** "Open windows, don't use switches, evacuate, call 0800 111 999"
2. Safety Latch: **Gas Emergency** (highest priority)
3. Contractor: **Gas Safe registered** (CKR1 for boilers)
4. Post-fix: Gas Safe certificate uploaded, CO alarm tested
5. **Never** clear hold without Gas Safe engineer

### Major Water Leak / Flood
1. Safety Latch: **Water + Electrics** if near electrical
2. Contractor: **Plumbing + Electrical** (or two contractors)
3. Priority: Stop water → Make safe → Dry → Repair
4. Document: Photos of damage, moisture readings
5. Insurance: Auto-creates claim reference if enabled

### Fire Damage
1. **999 first** — MaintenanceOS never delays fire response
2. Safety Latch: **Fire Safety** (structural, electrical, smoke)
3. Contractor: **Fire restoration specialist** + **Structural engineer** if needed
4. Post-fix: Fire risk assessment review, EICR, building control notification

### Structural Instability
1. Safety Latch: **Structural**
2. **Evacuate if imminent risk** — 999
3. Contractor: **Structural engineer** + **Groundworks**
4. Building Control notification required
5. No re-entry until engineer certifies safe

### No Heating in Winter (Oct–Mar)
1. Safety Latch: **Winter Heating** (if tenant vulnerable)
2. Contractor: **Gas Safe / OFTEC** (heating engineer)
3. SLA: **4 hours** (vulnerable), **24 hours** (standard)
4. Temporary heaters provided if >24 hours (policy dependent)

## Out-of-Hours Coverage

MaintenanceOS is pre-launch. There is no dedicated 24/7 support desk and no published out-of-hours SLA on this site. Not a live support desk.

A contractor emergency rota is a workspace setting you configure yourself. It is not a MaintenanceOS-staffed desk.

## Emergency Spend Authority

| Role | Limit (per repair) | Approval |
|------|-------------------|----------|
| Safety Officer | £2,000 | Self-authorised (note required) |
| Admin | £5,000 | Self-authorised |
| Manager | £500 | Requires Admin approval |
| Auto-approve (config) | £250 | None |

**Above limits:** Emergency authorisation via Slack/Teams/email to Admin — reply "APPROVE [amount]" within 15 minutes.

## Communication Templates

**Tenant notification (auto):**
> "EMERGENCY REPAIR: We've received a report of [issue] at your property. Emergency services [have been/are being] contacted. A qualified contractor will attend within [timeframe]. Your safety is our priority. Reply HELP for assistance."

**Contractor brief (auto):**
> "EMERGENCY JOB: [Property address] — [Issue description]. Safety Latch: [type]. Tenant: [name, phone, vulnerability flags]. Access: [instructions]. Required cert: [Gas Safe/NICEIC/etc.]. Target on-site: [time]. Reply ACK to accept."

## After-Action Review

Every emergency repair triggers a review (within 7 days):
1. Timeline reconstruction
2. Communication effectiveness
3. Contractor performance
4. Tenant outcome
5. Process improvements
6. Record in **Reports → Safety Incidents → After-Action**
