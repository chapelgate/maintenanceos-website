## Public emergency services

For life safety, use public emergency services. These are national services, not MaintenanceOS product features:

| Situation | Public number |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Gas emergency** | 0800 111 999 (National Gas Emergency Service) |
| **Fire or immediate danger** | 999 |

Do not treat this help page as a monitored desk.
