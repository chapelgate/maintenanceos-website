# Configuring Contractors

Your contractor network is the backbone of maintenance delivery. MaintenanceOS lets you manage contractors, their specialisms, and performance in one place.

## Adding a Contractor

Go to **Settings → Contractors → Add Contractor**.

**Required fields:**
- Company name
- Primary contact (name, phone, email)
- Trade specialisms (select all that apply)
- Coverage area (postcode districts or radius from base)

**Optional fields:**
- Insurance expiry date (MaintenanceOS will warn 30 days before expiry)
- Gas Safe registration number
- NICEIC / ELECSA registration number
- Preferred payment terms
- Notes (internal only)

## Trade Specialisms

Select from the standard list or add custom trades:

- Plumbing & Heating
- Electrical
- Gas Engineering
- Roofing
- Damp Proofing
- General Building
- Carpentry & Joinery
- Plastering & Rendering
- Groundworks & Drainage
- Pest Control
- Fire Safety
- Asbestos Management
- Lift Maintenance
- Cleaning & Clearance

Contractors can have multiple specialisms. When a repair is created, MaintenanceOS suggests contractors matching the repair category and coverage area.

## SLA Configuration

Set default SLA targets per trade:

| Trade | Emergency | Urgent | Standard |
|-------|-----------|--------|----------|
| Gas Engineering | 2 hours | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| Electrical | 2 hours | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| Plumbing | 4 hours | 8 hours | 48 hours |
| Roofing | 4 hours | 24 hours | 72 hours |
| General Building | 8 hours | 24 hours | 5 days |

These are workspace defaults. You can override per contractor or per property.

## Contractor Portal Access

Contractors receive a secure portal link where they can:
- View assigned repairs
- Update status (En Route / On Site / Completed / Needs Parts)
- Upload photos and notes
- Submit quotes for approval
- See their performance dashboard

Access is granted automatically when you assign their first repair.

## Performance Tracking

MaintenanceOS tracks:
- **First-time fix rate** (completed on first visit)
- **Average response time** (acknowledgement to on-site)
- **Quote accuracy** (quoted vs final cost)
- **Tenant satisfaction** (post-repair survey)
- **Compliance** (certificates up to date)

View reports at **Reports → Contractor Performance**.

## Bulk Import

For large networks, use the CSV import template (download from the Contractors page). Required columns:
- company_name, contact_name, email, phone, trades, postcode_districts
