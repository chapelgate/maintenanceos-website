# Managing Contractor Compliance

MaintenanceOS tracks contractor certifications, insurance, and qualifications to ensure only qualified contractors work on your properties.

## Tracked Certifications

| Certification | Trade | Expiry Check | Verification |
|---------------|-------|--------------|--------------|
| **Gas Safe Register** | Gas Engineering | Annual | Online API check |
| **NICEIC / ELECSA** | Electrical | Annual | Certificate upload |
| **OFTEC** | Oil Heating | Annual | Certificate upload |
| **F-Gas** | Air Conditioning | 3–5 years | Certificate upload |
| **Asbestos Licence** | Asbestos Removal | 1–3 years | HSE register check |
| **PAS 2030/2035** | Retrofit/Insulation | Annual | TrustMark register |
| **IPAF / PASMA** | Access Equipment | 5 years | Card upload |
| **First Aid at Work** | All (recommended) | 3 years | Certificate upload |

## Insurance Tracking

Required insurances (configurable minimums):
- **Public Liability** — Minimum £2M (default £5M)
- **Employers' Liability** — Minimum £5M (legal requirement)
- **Professional Indemnity** — If providing design/specification
- **Contract Works** — For major projects

## Automated Monitoring

**30 days before expiry:** Email to contractor + Manager
**7 days before expiry:** SMS to contractor + Manager + Admin
**On expiry date:** Contractor **suspended** from new assignments
**7 days after expiry:** Contractor **archived** (hidden from assignment)

Suspended contractors:
- Cannot be assigned new repairs
- Can complete currently assigned repairs
- Show as "Compliance Expired" in directory

## Renewal Workflow

1. Contractor uploads new certificate in portal
2. Manager/Admin reviews (auto-verified for Gas Safe via API)
3. Approve → Expiry date updated → Contractor reactivated
4. Reject → Contractor notified with reason → Re-upload

## Bulk Compliance Import

For onboarding large networks:
- CSV template with: contractor_id, cert_type, cert_number, expiry_date, document_url
- System validates format and creates renewal reminders

## Compliance Reports

**Dashboard:** Settings → Contractors → Compliance Overview
- Green: All current
- Amber: Expiring within 30 days
- Red: Expired or suspended

**Export:** Reports → Contractor Compliance
- Full list with expiry dates
- Filter by trade, status, property portfolio
- Regulator-ready format

## Property-Specific Requirements

Some properties need additional compliance:
- **High-rise (18m+):** Contractor must have Building Safety Act competence
- **HMO:** Enhanced fire safety awareness training
- **Schools/Healthcare:** DBS checks for contractors
- **Heritage:** Conservation-accredited trades

Configure at Property → Compliance Requirements.
