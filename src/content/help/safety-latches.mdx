# Safety Latches Explained

Safety latches are MaintenanceOS's core safety mechanism. They automatically detect high-risk repairs and prevent them from being assigned to contractors until a designated safety officer reviews them.

## What Is a Safety Latch?

A safety latch is an **automatic hold** placed on a repair when specific danger conditions are detected. It cannot be bypassed by managers or contractors — only a Safety Officer or Admin can clear it.

## Default Safety Rules

MaintenanceOS ships with these non-configurable safety rules:

### 1. Gas & Carbon Monoxide
**Triggers on:** Keywords "gas smell", "carbon monoxide", "CO alarm", "boiler leaking gas", "gas leak"
**Action:** Immediate Safety Hold + SMS to all Admins + Safety Officer
**Regulation:** Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998

### 2. No Heating in Winter
**Triggers on:** Category "Heating & Hot Water" + date between 1 Oct – 31 Mar + tenant vulnerability flag
**Action:** Safety Hold (Urgent priority) + notification to Manager + Safety Officer
**Target:** On site within 4 hours
**Regulation:** Homes (Fitness for Human Habitation) Act 2018

### 3. Water Near Electrics
**Triggers on:** Category "Plumbing" + keywords "electrical", "socket", "light fitting", "consumer unit", "fuse board"
**Action:** Safety Hold + immediate escalation
**Regulation:** Electricity at Work Regulations 1989

### 4. Structural Instability
**Triggers on:** Category "Structural" + keywords "crack", "bulge", "leaning", "subsidence", "collapse"
**Action:** Safety Hold + immediate escalation
**Regulation:** Building Regulations 2010, Part A

### 5. Fire Safety Equipment
**Triggers on:** Category "Fire Safety" + keywords "fire alarm", "extinguisher", "fire door", "emergency lighting", "smoke detector"
**Action:** Safety Hold (24-hour SLA)
**Regulation:** Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005

### 6. Asbestos Disturbance
**Triggers on:** Keywords "asbestos", "artex", "pipe lagging", "ceiling tiles" + category "Structural" or "Plumbing"
**Action:** Safety Hold + specialist contractor required
**Regulation:** Control of Asbestos Regulations 2012

### 7. Legionella Risk
**Triggers on:** Category "Plumbing" + keywords "stagnant", "dead leg", "cold water tank", "shower not used"
**Action:** Safety Hold + risk assessment required
**Regulation:** ACOP L8, HSG274

## How the Safety Latch Flow Works

```
1. Repair Reported
       ↓
2. Auto-Triage (keywords + category + property flags)
       ↓
3. Safety Rule Match? → YES → SAFETY HOLD
       ↓                                    ↓
   NO                                     Safety Officer Notified
       ↓                                    ↓
4. Normal Priority                    Safety Officer Reviews
       ↓                                    ↓
5. Assign Contractor              Clear Hold OR Escalate
       ↓                                    ↓
6. Continue Normal Flow           If Escalated → Specialist Required
```

## Safety Officer Role

**Who can be a Safety Officer:** Any Admin or Manager with the "Safety Officer" permission enabled.

**Responsibilities:**
- Review Safety Hold repairs within 30 minutes (emergency) or 2 hours (urgent)
- Verify contractor has correct certifications (Gas Safe, NICEIC, etc.)
- Authorise emergency spend limits
- Document decision rationale (audit requirement)

**Clearing a hold:**
1. Open repair → Safety Hold banner → "Review"
2. Confirm contractor certifications match risk
3. Add mandatory note: "Cleared: [reason], contractor [name] certified for [trade]"
4. Click "Clear Hold" → Repair moves to Assigned

## What Happens If No Safety Officer Is Available?

- After 30 minutes (emergency) or 2 hours (urgent), escalates to all Admins
- After 1 hour (emergency) or 4 hours (urgent), sends SMS to all Admins
- Repair remains in Safety Hold until cleared — **never auto-clears**

## Custom Safety Rules (Admin Only)

Admins can add **additional** rules (cannot remove defaults):
- Property-specific risks (e.g., known RAAC concrete)
- Tenant-specific vulnerabilities
- Seasonal risks (e.g., frozen pipes alert)

Custom rules follow the same clearance process.

## Audit & Compliance

Every Safety Hold creates an immutable record:
- Triggered rule
- Timestamp
- Safety Officer who cleared
- Clearance note
- Contractor assigned post-clearance

Export at **Reports → Safety Latch Audit** for regulator requests.

## Testing Safety Latches

Use the "Test Safety Latch" tool at Settings → Safety → Test Rules. Enter a test description and see which rules would trigger.
