# Fire Risk Assessments

The Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 requires a "suitable and sufficient" fire risk assessment (FRA) for all non-domestic premises and common parts of residential buildings.

## Who Needs an FRA

| Building Type | Requirement | Frequency |
|---------------|-------------|-----------|
| **Common parts of flats/HMOs** | Mandatory | Annual review, new FRA every 1–3 years |
| **Commercial premises** | Mandatory | Annual review |
| **Mixed-use (shop + flat)** | Mandatory for common parts | Annual review |
| **Single-family lets** | Not legally required | Recommended best practice |
| **Holiday lets** | Mandatory (as business premises) | Annual review |

## FRA in MaintenanceOS

Each property → Fire Safety tab:
- Current FRA: date, assessor, expiry/review date
- **Auto-warning:** 3 months before review due
- Significant findings tracked as remediation tasks
- Document storage (PDF, photos, floor plans)

## Creating an FRA Record

1. Property → Fire Safety → **New Assessment**
2. Enter:
   - Assessor name + company + competency evidence
   - Assessment date
   - Building description (use, occupancy, layout)
   - Significant findings (hazard, risk level, action, deadline)
   - Recommendations (priority: Immediate / High / Medium / Low)
3. Upload PDF report
4. System creates remediation tasks from findings

## Significant Findings → Tasks

Each finding becomes a tracked task:
- **Immediate:** Safety latch created, 24-hour target
- **High:** 30-day target, assigned to contractor
- **Medium:** 90-day target
- **Low:** 12-month target / next review

Tasks link back to FRA for audit trail.

## Common FRA Findings for Residential

| Hazard | Typical Action | Priority |
|--------|----------------|----------|
| Missing smoke alarms | Install Grade D LD2 system | Immediate |
| Fire doors not self-closing | Fit closers, intumescent strips | High |
| No fire blanket in kitchen | Install BS EN 1869 blanket | High |
| Blocked escape routes | Clear, signage, enforcement | Immediate |
| Damaged fire doors | Replace (FD30s) | High |
| No emergency lighting (3+ storeys) | Install BS 5266 system | High |
| Faulty fire alarm panel | Repair/service | High |
| Combustible storage in corridors | Remove, policy enforcement | Medium |

## Review vs New Assessment

- **Review:** Annual desktop check — any changes? (layout, occupancy, legislation)
- **New FRA:** Required if: major works, change of use, fire incident, 3+ years since last full FRA

MaintenanceOS prompts for review annually, new FRA every 3 years.

## Competent Person

Assessor must be "competent" — MaintenanceOS checks:
- Professional body membership (IFE, FIA, NEBOSH)
- Relevant experience (building type)
- Professional indemnity insurance
- Sample report quality check (optional)

## Multi-Building Portfolios

**Reports → Fire Safety Portfolio:**
- All properties with FRA status
- Filter: Overdue / Due Soon / Current
- Aggregate remediation tasks by priority
- Cost estimates for outstanding works
- Export for insurer / regulator

## Golden Thread (Building Safety Act)

For **higher-risk buildings (18m+ / 7+ storeys):**
- FRA part of "golden thread" of information
- Digital record required
- MaintenanceOS export meets structured data requirements
- Links to building safety case report
