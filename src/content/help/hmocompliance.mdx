# HMO Licensing & Compliance

Houses in Multiple Occupation (HMOs) require mandatory licensing and compliance with enhanced standards. MaintenanceOS tracks licences, conditions, and inspection readiness.

## What Counts as an HMO

**Mandatory licensing (England & Wales):**
- 5+ occupants forming 2+ households
- 3+ storeys (pre-2018 definition — some councils still apply)

**Additional licensing (council-specific):**
- Some councils license all HMOs regardless of size
- Check local scheme — MaintenanceOS flags by postcode

**Selective licensing (area-based):**
- All rental properties in designated areas
- Not HMO-specific but same tracking

## Licence Tracking

Each HMO property → HMO tab:
- Licence number + issuing council
- Issue date + expiry date (typically 5 years)
- Licence holder (must be "fit and proper person")
- Maximum permitted occupants
- **Auto-warning:** 6 months before expiry

## Mandatory Licence Conditions

Every HMO licence includes these conditions (Housing Act 2004):

### 1. Gas Safety
- Annual gas safety check (CP12)
- Copy to council on request

### 2. Electrical Safety
- 5-yearly EICR (Electrical Installation Condition Report)
- Portable appliance testing (PAT) recommended annually

### 3. Fire Safety
- Working smoke alarms (Grade D, LD2 coverage)
- Heat detector in kitchen
- Fire doors (FD30s) on risk rooms
- Fire blanket in kitchen
- Emergency lighting (if 3+ storeys)
- Fire risk assessment (annual review)

### 4. Amenities Standards
- Sufficient bathrooms/WCs for occupants
- Sufficient kitchen facilities
- Adequate heating, ventilation, lighting

### 5. Management Standards
- Manager contact details displayed
- Repair reporting system
- Refuse storage / collection
- Anti-social behaviour procedure

### 6. Room Sizes (England)
- Single room: ≥6.51m² (1 person), ≥10.22m² (2 persons)
- Double room: ≥10.22m²
- Under-10 rooms: ≥4.64m² (not counted for max occupancy)

## Condition Monitoring

MaintenanceOS creates **compliance tasks** for each condition:
- Gas safety → Annual task
- EICR → 5-yearly task
- Fire risk assessment → Annual task
- Smoke alarm test → Monthly task (contractor/tenant)
- Fire door inspection → Quarterly task

**Dashboard:** Compliance → HMO Conditions
- Green: Current
- Amber: Due within 30 days
- Red: Overdue

## Council Inspections

**Pre-inspection checklist** (auto-generated 2 weeks before):
- All certificates current
- Fire risk assessment reviewed
- Room sizes verified
- Amenities adequate
- Management records up to date
- Tenant feedback log available

**During inspection:**
- Council officer may request MaintenanceOS access (read-only)
- Use **Inspection Mode** → Generates compliance pack (PDF)

**Post-inspection:**
- Upload inspection report
- Track required actions with deadlines
- Evidence completion → Council sign-off

## Licence Renewal

Start 6 months before expiry:
1. Council application (MaintenanceOS provides data export)
2. Fit and proper person declaration
3. Updated fire risk assessment
4. Current gas/EICR certificates
5. Fee payment (varies by council, typically £500–£2,000)

## Penalties for Non-Compliance

- **Civil penalty:** Up to £30,000 per offence
- **Rent repayment order:** Up to 12 months' rent
- **Banning order:** Prevents letting
- **Criminal prosecution:** Unlimited fine
