# Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions. Can't find yours? [Contact support](./getting-help).

## General

### What is MaintenanceOS?
MaintenanceOS is UK maintenance operations software for property teams. It handles repair intake, contractor management, compliance tracking, and safety — all in one platform. The AI receptionist capability is under testing; fixed rules and authorised human clearance keep control of safety and money.

### Is MaintenanceOS generally available?
**No.** We are in **pre-launch early access** with design partners. Not commercially generally available; not a public always-on phone service. Features and pricing may change.

### Who is MaintenanceOS for?
UK landlords, letting agents, property managers, housing associations, maintenance companies, councils, facilities management firms, and holiday let operators. Any team managing UK property maintenance.

### What makes MaintenanceOS different?
- **Safety-first:** Fixed safety latches that cannot be bypassed
- **Compliance-built-in:** Gas, EPC, Fire, HMO tracking automated
- **Contractor network:** Managed directory with performance tracking
- **Tenant portal:** Self-service repair reporting
- **API-first:** Integrate with your existing stack
- **Honest AI:** No fake availability claims — rules and people keep control

## Features

### Does MaintenanceOS have a phone number for tenants?
**Not a public always-on phone.** The AI receptionist capability is under testing. Tenants report repairs via portal, email, or your existing phone. Emergency safety latches trigger immediate human escalation.

### Can I use my existing contractors?
Yes. Add them to your contractor directory. They get portal access for assigned repairs only.

### Does it integrate with my property management software?
Yes. Pre-built: Reapit, AgentOS, Qube, MRI, Yardi. Custom: REST API + webhooks.

### Is there a mobile app?
**Progressive Web App (PWA)** — Install from browser, works offline, push notifications. Native iOS/Android apps planned post-launch.

### Can tenants report repairs in Welsh?
Yes. Full Cymraeg translation in tenant portal (toggle in header).

## Compliance

### Does MaintenanceOS guarantee compliance?
**No software can guarantee compliance.** MaintenanceOS automates tracking, scheduling, warnings, and audit trails. You remain responsible for legal obligations. See `/compliance/` for details.

### Which regulations does it cover?
- Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998
- Energy Performance of Buildings Regulations (MEES)
- Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005
- Housing Act 2004 (HMO Licensing)
- Building Safety Act 2022 (golden thread)
- Electrical Safety Standards 2020
- Homes (Fitness for Human Habitation) Act 2018

### Can I track custom compliance requirements?
Yes. Add custom compliance types with custom schedules, certificates, and warnings.

## Data & Security

### Where is my data stored?
**UK only (London).** DigitalOcean LON1, Cloudflare LHR. Enterprise: UK-only contractual guarantee.

### Who owns my data?
**You.** MaintenanceOS is a data processor; you are the controller. DPA included in Terms.

### Can I export my data?
Yes. Settings → Billing → Export Data (CSV/JSON). API bulk export for Enterprise.

### Is it GDPR compliant?
Designed for UK GDPR compliance. DPO: dpo@chapelgatetechnologies.com. See `/privacy/` and `/security/`.
