# Specification: WorkSmartOS bid-ready website

## Objective
Make the public WorkSmartOS site usable in a contract bid: the create-flow website check must finish or fail in a way the visitor can continue, a partial scan must say what was found and what was not, and the scanner image must match the droplet that is already live.

## Brand
WorkSmartOS is the brand. MaintenanceOS and ReceptionistOS are products under it. Visitor-facing legal pages say WorkSmartOS website. The company remains Chapelgate Limited (12340004). Colours stay navy `#0A1728`, cyan `#67E8F9`, type Inter.

## Acceptance criteria
- `/create` calls `POST /api/website/scan`. A successful scan fills only returned fields. A zero-fetch `PARTIAL` (`fetched: 0`) tells the visitor the pages could not be read and lets them continue. Timeout and 5xx retry once. A 4xx is not retried. The visitor can skip the check while it is running.
- The droplet scan stops at about 18 seconds, persists the crawl, and returns HTTP 200 with `scannerV2.state` `CRAWLING` plus any draft and facts already read. That budget covers the whole request, including resume loading, document parsing, renderer time, and the final draft build. A hard timer returns the persisted partial, and a slow draft build still answers inside 18 seconds. PDF and DOC parsing is limited to a short slice, and a parse failure is skipped. The website function never returns 5xx: a lost or timed-out upstream call is HTTP 200 `{ ok: true, scannerV2: { state: "CRAWLING", crawlId }, warnings: ["scan_gateway_retry"] }`. The crawl id is the one the client sent. When the client sent none, it is the deterministic `scanner-v2:gateway:` id for that origin. A real crawl id is never replaced. `/create` keeps the richest draft across continues, shows "Still reading your site (n pages)", and stops after 8 continues with that draft and a partial notice. An origin-only `SCANNER_V2_RUNTIME_URL` is called at `/v1/onboarding/scan`.
- A `PARTIAL` result with `fetched > 0` names the draft fields that were copied and the fields that are still blank, on the check step and the review step. The visitor can edit the blanks.
- The scanner's plain HTTPS fetch sends an honest browser-like user agent, `Accept`, and `Accept-Language`. It does not send `Authorization` or `Cookie`. A 401 or 403 on that fetch retries the document through Playwright after the public-HTTPS and DNS checks. A 404 does not. Tests cover a host that returns 401 for a bot user agent and 200 for the browser headers, and a host that 401s the plain client until the browser fetch.
- The scanner image is `mcr.microsoft.com/playwright:v1.63.0-noble`, runs as uid 1000, and reads `SCANNER_V2_STATE_ROOT=/data`. Deploy docs and `deploy/maintenanceos-scanner-v2/run.sh` use `--user 1000:1000` and mount `/var/lib/maintenanceos-scanner-v2` at `/data`. There is no `node` user and no `SCANNER_V2_STATE_DIR` setting.
- A scan copies a public business phone, a public contact email, and a postal or contact-block address when they are on the page, with source URL and confidence. Mailto links, visible text, JSON-LD, and the contact or footer are read, including simple obfuscation (`name [at] domain`, `(at)`, HTML entities, and Cloudflare `data-cfemail`). Script-built emails and personal addresses from blog, news, jobs, or team pages are not reconstructed. A postal address uses street, locality, and postcode, and omits region or country codes such as ENG and GB. The locality on the page is kept. Services are short list items under a services heading; slogan and call-to-action headings are dropped. A service line that contains an email, phone, postcode, URL, or a navigation label such as Contact, About, Our history, or Request a valuation is dropped. FAQ pairs come only from FAQPage markup, or from at least three details or accordion questions that end in a question mark. A sentence, a name longer than about 60 characters, or a trade-label title such as Car Servicing Wirral or Wirral Plumber loses to a JSON-LD name, `og:site_name`, a copyright line, or the domain brand (`croftautomotive` becomes Croft Automotive, `wirralheating` becomes Wirral Heating). The brand is the text before `are`/`is`/`we`/`provide`, or the title segment before `|`, `-`, or `–`. A 404, not-found, error, home, about, welcome, or untitled title is rejected, as is a name that is only the domain slug. If none of those names survive, the draft uses a title-cased domain at low confidence. A service area that is a trade plus a place, such as Plumbers Liverpool, is dropped. Date and byline answers are dropped. Every draft field is entity-decoded and tag-stripped.
- Phone, email, address, and hours are trusted from the home page or a shallow contact or opening-hours page. When a crawl has several distinct candidates, or more than eight pages, other pages are ignored. Email, address, and hours keep the most prominent value. A draft keeps up to two phone numbers, primary first. Confidence is low when non-site numbers were discarded. An address must contain a UK postcode, or a house number plus a street word. Navigation labels such as Back, Home, Menu, and Contact are rejected. A footer address anchors on the UK postcode, drops headings and junk before the house number (FIND US, Address, Cancellation Policy, Talk to us), and keeps the street, estate, locality, and postcode. When that postcode matches schema, the footer or visible address is kept and the source is recorded. Opening hours are parsed as HTML, then cut at the first line that is not hours. Titles lose a leading "Welcome to". Hostnames and menu labels such as Back, Menu, Search, and Skip to content are not the business name or a service.
- The first scan keeps reading inside about 45 seconds, priority pages first, including contact, about, and services when the home page has no contact facts. A crawl that fetched pages and found no phone, email, address, hours, or services is `PARTIAL` with warning `thin_home_page`, not `COMPLETE`. `/create` repeats the scan up to eight times while the state is `CRAWLING`, shows "Still reading your site (n pages)", and keeps the draft with the most filled fields if a later response is empty. After that limit it shows the best draft and a partial notice. The Continue scanning button remains. A `CRAWLING` result with a large discovered count asks for the contact or services page URL. `blocked_by_site` is unchanged.
- `/create` shows phone, contact email, and address as editable review fields. The phone field can hold the primary and secondary numbers. `http_404` and `content_type_not_supported` are a quiet "Some pages were skipped." note. State `CRAWLING` keeps the partial draft usable and offers Continue scanning, which sends the same website again. `blocked_by_site` is unchanged.
- Spoken demo lines try `POST /api/website/voice` and fall back to the browser voice. The function calls Gemini `generateContent` with `gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts` (override `GEMINI_TTS_MODEL`), `responseModalities: ["AUDIO"]`, and prebuilt voice `Charon` (override `GEMINI_TTS_VOICE`; Algenib is the gravelly alternative). Google lists Charon as Informative. The page name stays Theatrical. The only secret is `GEMINI_API_KEY`, sent in `x-goog-api-key` and never in a URL, a log, or the browser. PCM comes back as `audio/wav`. A missing key, Gemini error, HTTP 429, or a timeout returns `fallback: "browser"`.

## Non-goals
- Changing frozen telephony.
- Adding `/status` to the sitemap.
- Inventing business facts when a page cannot be read.
