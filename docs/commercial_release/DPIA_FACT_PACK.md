## Collection Sources and Flows

- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Internal JSON API creates work orders through `api.py:create_work_order`, guarded by `api.py:require_internal_api`, bounded text validation, tenant checks, and `channels.py:create_from_channel`.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Contractor portal forms collect contractor decisions, availability, completion notes, evidence URLs, quotes, and invoices through `api.py` routes `/contractor/decision`, `/contractor/availability`, `/contractor/complete`, `/quotes`, and `/invoices`, then write through `operations.py`.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Resident confirmation and appointment confirmation data is collected through `api.py:confirm` and `api.py:appointment_confirm`, then persisted through `operations.py:resident_confirmation` and `operations.py:confirm_appointment`.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Twilio SMS/WhatsApp inbound messages are collected by `api.py:messaging_inbound` after public-ingress and Twilio-signature checks; matched residents flow to `channels.py:create_from_channel`, and unmatched messages flow to `UnmatchedInbound`.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Twilio voice call entry is collected by `api.py:voice_inbound` after public-ingress and Twilio-signature checks; it creates a `CallSession` through `voice_bridge.py:create_call_session`, admits capacity, and returns TwiML for the media stream when provider readiness passes.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Twilio WebSocket media streams enter through `api.py:voice_media_stream` and `provider_runtime.py:handle_provider_media_stream`, which dispatches only to allowlisted `openai` or `gemini` adapters in `provider_runtime.py`.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: IMAP email ingestion exists in `email_gateway.py:fetch_unseen`; it reads unseen messages and returns message id, sender, recipient, subject, and plain-text body. I did not find a canonical production scheduler in the inspected files that invokes this path.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Outbound communication queuing hashes recipients before storing them in `communications.participant` through `communications.py:queue_outbound` and records provider delivery state through `communications.py:record_delivery_status`.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Internal voice call reports are generated by `call_notifications.py:dispatch_internal_call_email`; they parse persisted transcripts, fetch Twilio caller/called numbers after the call, queue an email, and then send via `email_gateway.py:send_email`.

## Processor and Interface Boundaries

- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Phone carrier boundary is Twilio only. `provider_webhooks.py` defines `SUPPORTED_PHONE_PROVIDERS = {"twilio"}` and mutates Twilio incoming-number URLs only through `apply_twilio_incoming_phone_voice_url` after fail-closed readiness checks.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Realtime AI voice provider boundary is allowlisted to `openai` and `gemini` in `provider_runtime.py:SUPPORTED_PROVIDERS` and `_provider_adapters`.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: OpenAI Realtime receives Twilio media payloads and sends audio/transcription events through `voice_bridge.py:handle_twilio_media_stream`, `_openai_connect`, and the session configuration in `_configure_openai_session`. It uses `OPENAI_API_KEY`, `OPENAI_REALTIME_MODEL`, `OPENAI_TRANSCRIPTION_MODEL`, and `OPENAI_REALTIME_VOICE`.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Gemini Live receives transcoded Twilio audio and returns model audio/transcriptions through `gemini_voice.py:handle_gemini_media_stream`, `genai.Client`, and `types.LiveConnectConfig` with input/output audio transcription enabled. It uses `GEMINI_API_KEY` or `GOOGLE_API_KEY`.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Resend HTTPS and SMTP are outbound email processors in `email_gateway.py:send_email`. Resend is preferred when a Resend credential is available; SMTP fallback is disabled for voice/completion dispatch unless explicitly allowed.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Twilio REST is used after calls by `call_notifications.py:call_numbers_for_call_sid` to fetch caller ANI and dialled number, and by `voice_bridge.py:_request_live_human_transfer` to update a call with `<Dial>`.
- `UNKNOWN`: External provider retention, training, regional processing, subcontractors, or contractual roles are not implemented or evidenced in repository source. `DATA_GOVERNANCE.md` marks provider contracts and international transfers as items requiring professional approval.

- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Tenant-scoped work-order CSV export exists in `export_csv.py:export_work_orders`; it requires a positive `client_id` and writes only `WorkOrder` fields for that tenant.
- `UNKNOWN`: I found no implemented full DSAR/export surface for residents, contractors, call transcripts, communications, audit events, or deletion/correction request workflows. `DATA_GOVERNANCE.md` requires reviewed access, correction, deletion, transcript access, and export controls before real data.

## Deletion and Retention Implementation Status

- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: SQLAlchemy invariants prevent deletion of active critical-safety state and work orders with active critical safety in `models.py:enforce_work_order_invariants`.
- `DESIGN INTENT`: `DATA_GOVERNANCE.md` requires retention per data class and legal/operational hold, scheduled deletion, independent deletion testing, and preservation of safety/audit records where holds apply.
- `UNKNOWN`: I found no production retention scheduler, deletion job, legal-hold table/workflow, transcript deletion workflow, backup deletion workflow, or external evidence-store retention implementation. This matches the explicit blocker in `DATA_GOVERNANCE.md`.

## Tenant Boundary

- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Most primary tables carry `client_id` directly or indirectly through work-order/property/resident links in `models.py`.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: `tenant_security.py:require_client_resource` rejects cross-tenant property, resident, contractor, and work-order access. Internal API tenant readiness is checked by `tenant_security.py:internal_api_tenant_ready` and `api.py:require_internal_api`.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Voice calls require an isolated synthetic tenant. `tenant_security.py:configured_voice_client_id`, `isolated_voice_tenant_ready`, and `bind_call_to_tenant` require `VOICE_TENANT_MODE=isolated_test`, `VOICE_TEST_CLIENT_ID`, and a `TenantBoundary` with mode `SYNTHETIC_TEST`.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Call sessions are bound to tenants through `CallSessionTenant`; `voice_bridge.py:create_call_session` binds the call and refuses a foreign existing session by re-checking `bind_call_to_tenant`.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: `README.md` states implemented product controls include tenant-scoped properties, residents, contractors, work orders, links, exports, jobs, communications, action evidence, and call sessions.
- `UNKNOWN`: Production multi-tenant administrative access controls, MFA/session policy, and operator access reviews are outside the inspected implemented tenant-boundary code. `docs/commercial_release/MASTER_RELEASE_PROBLEMS.md` lists production identity/MFA/RBAC/session controls as incomplete.

## Transcript and Audio Persistence

- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Voice transcripts are persisted in `CallSession.transcript` by `voice_bridge.py:record_transcript_segment`; Gemini calls `_persist_segment`, which delegates to `voice_bridge.record_transcript_segment`.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Call-session closure is handled by `voice_bridge.py:close_call_session`, which can create unmatched inbound records or dispatch internal call email via `call_notifications.py`.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: OpenAI assistant audio is buffered in memory in `voice_bridge.py:handle_twilio_media_stream` until matching output transcript passes policy checks; audio payloads are sent to Twilio as media frames and cleared from in-memory buffers.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Gemini audio is transcoded between Twilio mulaw and Gemini PCM in `gemini_voice.py` and sent as WebSocket media frames; transcript buffers are persisted as text segments.
- `UNKNOWN`: I found no repository evidence that raw call audio is written to application database tables or local files in `voice_bridge.py` or `gemini_voice.py`. Provider-side Twilio recordings or AI-provider retention are not evidenced in source.

## Special-Category, Vulnerability, and Emergency Touchpoints

- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: The system processes safety and emergency indicators as operational data. `decision_engine.py` is described in `ARCHITECTURE.md` as deterministic safety classification before workflow logic, and `models.py:CriticalSafetyState` stores active flags, clearance user, clearance reason, and timestamps.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Voice prompts and deterministic checks include credible immediate threats to life/serious injury, animal-welfare emergency, safeguarding, vulnerable, harassment, abuse, legal/complaint terms, and emergency transfer rules in `voice_bridge.py` and `gemini_voice.py`.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Active critical safety forces `HUMAN_REVIEW`, `P1`, and preserved flags through `models.py:enforce_work_order_invariants`; clearance requires authorised audited workflow according to `models.py` and `safety_controls.py` as referenced by `api.py:safety_clearance`.
- `UNKNOWN`: No separate special-category classification table, privacy review workflow, or vulnerability-specific retention/access workflow was found. `DATA_GOVERNANCE.md` requires professional approval for purpose, transparency, incident response, provider contracts, and call-recording/monitoring notices before real people/properties.

## Human-vs-AI Authority Boundaries

- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: `README.md` states AI may collect/classify information but cannot override safety or perform consequential actions.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: `voice_policy.py` rejects prohibited financial confirmations, unverified private disclosures, critical-safety downgrades, and unverified action claims. It allowlists model actions and blocks consequential actions unless tenant context, idempotency, safety, and `ai_side_effects_enabled` conditions pass.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: `ARCHITECTURE.md` states only verified successful domain evidence can authorise affirmative claims, and `voice_bridge.py` buffers model audio until policy checks pass.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Human approvals are required for dispatch, quote, invoice, post-safety clearance, identity, triage, escalation, repeat-repair, evidence reuse, and contractor-timeout paths through `operations.py:APPROVAL_ROLES`, `operations.py:approve_operational_action`, and `operations.py:reject_operational_action`.
- `IMPLEMENTED FACT`: Critical safety cannot be cleared by caller minimisation, model output, resident confirmation, contractor action, approval, quote, invoice, completion, closure, retry, or direct ORM mutation according to `ARCHITECTURE.md` and enforced in `models.py:enforce_work_order_invariants`.
