# 39-gdpr-accountability — UK GDPR accountability

## Requirement
UK GDPR Articles 5(2), 24, 25, 30, 32-36: accountability, records of processing activities (ROPA), DPIA status, lawful basis determination, privacy notices, data retention policies, subject access request process, data breach notification process, international transfers, and ICO accountability framework.

Applies to: All 12 products
Source: release-standards-council-20261114/out/STANDARDS.md (items #39-#47 UK GDPR compliance, #40 DPIA, #42 retention, #44 SAR, #45 breach, #47 transfers)

Standard item in STANDARDS.md: see item number above

## How Chapelgate Meets It in Practice
This shadow template records the standard so Chapelgate operates to it day to day; buying certification later is a formality, not a rebuild. All 13 DPIA drafts in compliance/dpia/ are DRAFT — NOT SIGNED OFF. ROPA and consolidated documentation require gathering from existing product repos.
