My opening position: the framework should adopt a tiered evidence framework: mandatory primary sources for pricing and certifications, sampled validation for case studies. The cost of compliance is far lower than the cost of framework erosion. (~142 words)

I advise strict adherence to the Public Contracts Regulations 2015. My position: any substantiated claim without evidence should trigger not just disqualification, but potential legal action, especially if the public sector incurs costs or delays. However, we must distinguish between honest errors and deliberate deception. A clear, published "errors and omissions" process, coupled with a right to rebut, ensures fairness while maintaining deterrence. Compliance isn't optional—it's the foundation of the entire framework's legitimacy. (~141 words)
