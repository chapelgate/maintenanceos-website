# Chapelgate Technologies — Welsh Meeting Transcription Pilot

**Chapelgate turns Welsh and bilingual council meetings into searchable Welsh/English transcript drafts that officers check and publish, so Welsh councils can meet their Welsh Language Standards duties without adding translation staff. We start with a free six-week pilot.**
