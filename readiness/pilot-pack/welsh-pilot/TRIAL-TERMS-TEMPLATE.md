## 1. Scope

One recorded public meeting (council, committee, or full-council meeting) is transcribed using Welsh-language transcription provided by Chapelgate Multilingual Transcribe. The council supplies one public meeting recording (already on its public webcast or council-supplied non-sensitive recording). Chapelgate transcribes it once and returns one bilingual Welsh/English draft transcript.

## 2. What the council gets

A searchable Welsh/English transcript draft of the one recorded public meeting, for officer review only. No accuracy guarantee is given or implied. WER/CER figures are bench estimates only and are not a performance commitment for this meeting.

## 3. What Chapelgate gets

Permission to use the transcript as an anonymised case study only if the council agrees in writing afterwards. This is opt-in, and the council can say no at any time. If the council does not agree in writing, Chapelgate will not publish or use the transcript or any council-identifying details in any public material.

## 4. Data handling

Aligned with the DPIA draft for Chapelgate Multilingual Transcribe (jobs-work-dell/readiness/council-gates/dpia/chapelgate-multilingual-transcribe.md, version 1.0, DRAFT for council review, 2026-11-17):

- Public recordings only — one council-supplied public meeting recording, no personal data beyond what is already public in the meeting recording.
- UK processing on Chapelgate equipment — audio and transcript are processed on Chapelgate's own UK-based equipment (local CPU inference). No paid API, no external model provider.
- No model training on council data unless separately agreed in writing.
- Access limits — only the named Chapelgate pilot lead and the named council pilot lead can access the recording and transcript during the trial.
- Deletion by the end date — Chapelgate deletes the recording and all pilot data by the end date in section 7, and provides written confirmation of deletion. The council may also request deletion at any time, in writing, and Chapelgate will delete within 5 working days.

## 5. Cost

Free, with no obligation to buy. No licence fees, no per-minute charge, no production tenancy integration.

## 6. Either side can stop at any time

Either the council or Chapelgate may stop the trial at any time, for any reason, by written notice to the other side. On stopping, Chapelgate deletes all pilot data and provides written confirmation.

## 7. End date

Fixed end date: [date] = 30 days from Chapelgate's receipt of the council's recording, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing before that date.

## How we'll measure success

The three metrics below are fixed in writing before the first recording is sent:

**1. Turnaround.** Working hours from recording received to transcript draft returned, timed by both sides. The target is set at kickoff from the Dell bench timings for that recording length (bench basis recorded in out/METRICS-BASIS.md). Never quote a number the bench has not produced.

**2. Officer edit rate.** The percentage of transcript lines an officer changes before publishing, measured by a line diff between Chapelgate's draft and the council's published version (computed and shared by Chapelgate). Report Welsh and English lines separately. The target is set at kickoff and stated as a percentage.

**3. Run it again.** One yes/no question to the council lead at the end: "Would you run this again on your next meeting?" with an optional one-line reason.

**Baseline:** how long the council's current process takes for the same meeting, if the council will share it.

Each metric maps directly to a field in the Case Study Template (turnaround, edit rate, run-again plus the reason, baseline comparison). A case study is only published with the council's written opt-in. If the targets are missed, the results are still reported honestly to the council, and no public case study is made.
