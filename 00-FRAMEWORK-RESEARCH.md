# G-Cloud Framework Research

## Current iteration
G-Cloud 15 launched 10 August 2026. Consolidates 3 separate frameworks into a single commercial agreement. £14bn over 4 year term (~£3bn annual public sector cloud spend). Call-off contracts up to 60 months. Open framework format under Procurement Act 2023. New SMEs can join during an 18-month re-opening window after launch, and at regular intervals throughout the 4-year term.
Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/news/g-cloud-relaunches-with-biggest-upgrade-in-its-history (accessed 15 Nov 2026)

## Previous iteration (for reference)
G-Cloud 14: applications opened 19 Feb 2024, closed 7 May 2024, awarded 29 Oct 2024, operational until expiry 28 October 2026. Applications now closed.
Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/what-to-do-and-when-for-g-cloud-12 (accessed 15 Nov 2026)

## Application window
G-Cloud 15 launched 10 Aug 2026; framework open for public sector use later Aug 2026. New suppliers can join during an 18-month re-opening window after launch. Contract notices issued via Find a Tender; suppliers have 4-6 weeks to apply when notice issued; G-Cloud notices issued approx every 12 months.
Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/selling-on-the-digital-marketplace (accessed 15 Nov 2026)

## Eligibility
Suppliers apply to sell cloud services on the Digital Marketplace. Must create a supplier account at https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk. Evaluation on quality, technical proficiency and pricing.
Sources: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/selling-on-the-digital-marketplace; https://www.gov.uk/government/news/g-cloud-relaunches-with-biggest-upgrade-in-its-history (accessed 15 Nov 2026)

## Lots
G-Cloud lots cover cloud software and services (cloud software / cloud support / cloud hosting categories on Digital Marketplace). G-Cloud 15 consolidates Cloud Compute 2 and G-Cloud 14 Lots 1-3 and 4 into single agreement with modernised classification system.
Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/news/g-cloud-relaunches-with-biggest-upgrade-in-its-history (accessed 15 Nov 2026)

## Supplier declaration
Declaration questions answered during Digital Marketplace application. New suppliers cannot reuse prior responses; must answer each declaration question. Service question responses may be reused unless question changed.
Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/what-to-do-and-when-for-g-cloud-12 (accessed 15 Nov 2026)

## Required documents
Service definition documents per product, terms and conditions, pricing documents. Evidence: compliance pack, SBOM, pen test reports. Andy-only steps (account creation, accepting terms, supplier declaration, signing framework agreement, fee/spend, insurance) parked in PARKED.md and TENDER-REGISTER.md.

## Costs
Registration is free (confirm with source). Framework agreement / call-off contract templates published by CCS/GCA.
Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/g-cloud-templates-and-legal-documents (accessed 15 Nov 2026); https://www.gov.uk/guidance/selling-on-the-digital-marketplace (accessed 15 Nov 2026)

## Register interest route
Create supplier account on Digital Marketplace (https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk) and monitor Find a Tender for G-Cloud contract notice.
