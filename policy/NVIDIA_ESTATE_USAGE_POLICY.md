# NVIDIA Estate Usage Policy

Status: STRICT ESTATE-WIDE RULE

The Chapelgate estate is currently owner-designated non-commercial unless a specific workload is explicitly designated otherwise.

This designation does not override NVIDIA licence terms, trial terms, service limits, quotas, rate limits, production-use restrictions, model-specific licences, or other binding provider conditions.

Mandatory rule:

1. Fully utilise NVIDIA models and all available free NVIDIA capacity wherever the applicable NVIDIA terms permit it.
2. Prefer NVIDIA capacity for bounded engineering work when available and suitable.
3. Use permitted concurrency and parallelism aggressively, but stay within provider quotas, rate limits, entitlements and technical safeguards.
4. Never bypass, rotate around, defeat or evade NVIDIA limits, keys, quotas, entitlements, account restrictions or licence boundaries.
5. Never treat non-commercial status as proof that a developer or trial service is unrestricted.
6. Do not use an NVIDIA service for live production or end-user serving when that service's applicable terms prohibit production use.
7. Engineering outputs produced through permitted NVIDIA development workflows may move through the estate's normal verification and release process, subject to the licences applying to the output and target runtime.
8. Verify current model/service-specific terms before expanding a workload when permission is unclear.
9. Provider terms and estate safety boundaries override assumptions made by agents, prompts or orchestration logic.
10. No agent may weaken this rule merely to increase throughput.

Engineering intent: maximise useful NVIDIA capacity with the fewest self-imposed restrictions possible while remaining strictly inside the provider's actual permissions and the estate's production-engineering rules.
