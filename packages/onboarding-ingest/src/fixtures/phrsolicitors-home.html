Percy Hughes & Roberts Solicitors

Percy Hughes & Roberts Solicitors is here to help you. We provide clear, simple and cost-effective legal advice to both individuals and businesses across the North West, including Birkenhead, Wirral, Liverpool and Merseyside. We are truly dedicated to providing practical support to the community we serve.

From our offices in Birkenhead, our solicitors and other lawyers provide a wide spectrum of legal services in both personal and business law.
