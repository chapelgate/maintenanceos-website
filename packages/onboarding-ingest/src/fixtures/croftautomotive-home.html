Suspension Services

Bumpy rides are one of the tell-tale signs of a worn-out or damaged suspension system. If you are facing any such issues, it is time to visit us for a suspension inspection. You can schedule an appointment online or visit us for bookings. On the scheduled date, we will inspect all your vehicle’s suspension components and conduct the necessary repairs and replacements as required.

Wheel Balancing

Inadequate weight distribution across car wheels can jeopardise your driving experience and on-road safety. If you come across any signs of unbalanced car wheels like a vehicle pulling to one side, or uneven tyre tread wear, contact us.

We use an advanced wheel balancing machine to detect the point of imbalance accurately and add eco-friendly counterweights to restore the balance.

Exhaust Repair

Your vehicle’s exhaust system plays a crucial role in purifying the harmful gases produced during the combustion process. Thus, any malfunction in this component can affect your car’s emission level.

At Croft Automotive Group, we inspect exhaust components like catalytic converters, exhaust manifolds, oxygen sensors, muffler etc. while providing exhaust services. As it is not possible to repair most of the exhaust components, we replace the faulty ones with OE-grade spares after informing you about the costs. We will only start our work with your permission.
