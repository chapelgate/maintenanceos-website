WHY US?

We’re proud of our work and confident in our abilities, and we think you will be too.

Project done on time

When we quote for your boiler installation or anything other work, we will provide what we feel is a realistic and achievable time scale. If we foresee problems in delivering to the date we set, we will let you know in advance. This means our customers can set firm plans in place and know when we will need access to complete our work.

Always avaliable

Unfortunately, boiler break-downs and burst pipes can occur at any time. We try to make ourselves available to our customers all the time, and at the very least will always try to offer help and advice on the telephone. We know that central heating, hot water and plumbing are vital to any household, and so we are available for emergency call-outs.

Professional and responsible

We have more accreditations and certificates from boiler manufacturers than most central heating engineers in Wirral, ensuring that we can work with almost all systems. Being accredited also means that your boiler guarantees remain valid if we install, service or repair your boiler. We also believe in working professionally, meaning we won’t leave tools sitting around and will work as tidily as possible
